My plan collects the sections you choose, flags time conflicts, and lets you print the plan, save it as a PDF, or copy it as text for an email. It is kept only in your browser. About this list

The day and time filters keep a section only when all of its meetings fall on the days you pick and inside the time window. Sections with no set meeting time, such as online or TBD, always fit.

The 500-level courses are the graduate versions of cross-listed courses that the catalog also lists. The catalog's generic world language entries (WL 2xx to 4xx) are covered through the specific language courses it names.
