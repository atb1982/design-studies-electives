Sections and seats come from NC State Class Search as of the time shown above, so a section can fill after that. Restricted sections are often limited to certain majors or need departmental approval, so confirm with an advisor before planning around them.

Courses in the Advisor approval category are not on the catalog's elective lists but usually count with an advisor's approval. Special topics sections can change topic each semester, so check the topic first.

About this list

The day and time filters keep a section only when all of its meetings fall on the days you pick and inside the time window. Sections with no set meeting time, such as online or TBD, always fit.

The 500-level courses are the graduate versions of cross-listed courses that the catalog also lists. The catalog's generic world language entries (WL 2xx to 4xx) are covered through the specific language courses it names.

Sources: the Design Studies (BA) catalog page and NC State Class Search.
