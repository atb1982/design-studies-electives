Restricted sections are often limited to specific majors or need departmental approval, so confirm with an advisor before planning around them. The day and time filters keep only the sections whose meetings all fall on the days you pick and inside the time window. Sections with no set meeting time, such as online or TBD, always fit. Courses tagged "Advisor approval" are not on the catalog's elective lists but usually count toward the degree with an advisor's approval. Special topics sections can carry different topics each semester, so check the topic before you plan around one.
